## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Formulation

### 1.1 The Challenge of Modern Multimodal Disinformation

Modern digital misinformation rarely manifests as pure text or isolated imagery. Contemporary disinformation campaigns deploy hybrid, multimodal manipulation strategies:

1. **Out-of-Context Media ("Cheap Fakes"):** Factual, unaltered photographs repurposed alongside deceptive, sensationalist claims (e.g., pairing a calm photo of Venice canals with breaking disaster headlines).
2. **Deepfakes & Facial Manipulation:** Photorealistic synthetic faces or facial re-enactments (e.g., DeepFakes, Face2Face, FaceSwap, NeuralTextures) coupled with political or corporate claims.
3. **Synthetic / Generative AI Media:** Entirely fabricated visuals generated by Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs like StyleGAN) or Latent Diffusion Models (LDMs like Stable Diffusion / Midjourney) paired with conspiratorial narratives.
4. **Multimodal Coordinated Deception:** Tampered visuals combined with fabricated claims designed to reinforce each other.

Traditional unimodal detection systems suffer from severe structural blindspots:

- **Text-only detectors** cannot verify if an associated visual contradicts the textual claim.
- **Image-only detectors** fail when the image itself is authentic but used deceptively out-of-context.
- **Standard RGB Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)** often miss high-frequency upsampling artifacts left behind by generative decoders.
- **Black-box models** output opaque scores without attributing _why_ a post is deceitful, hindering human verification.

### 1.2 Core Research Hypotheses

This project systematically addresses these limitations by designing and evaluating three formal research hypotheses:

- **Hypothesis H1 (Cross-Modal Attention Superiority):** A learned bidirectional multi-head cross-attention mechanism between text and vision representations captures cross-modal dependencies and semantic inconsistencies superior to standard Early Fusion (feature concatenation) and Late Fusion (decision-level ensemble).
- **Hypothesis H2 (Forensic Frequency Advantage):** Integrating a dedicated 2D-FFT frequency-domain branch that captures periodic interpolation artifacts and high-frequency spectral deviations improves deepfake and synthetic image recall compared to RGB-only spatial backbones.
- **Hypothesis H3 (Explainable AI Grounding):** Providing multifaceted, grounded explanations (word-level attention attributions, 2D power spectrum heatmaps, and natural language synthesis) improves human interpretability and verification accuracy compared to bare label predictions.

## 2. 4-Way Output Classification Taxonomy

In compliance with PRD Section 4.2 & 6.5, the system categorizes any submitted text-image pair into one of four mutually exclusive, calibrated operational classes:

```
                                  Visual Content Authentic?
                                      YES               NO
                              ┌─────────────────┬─────────────────┐
                         YES  │ Likely          │ Potentially     │
    Text Claim                │ Authentic       │ Manipulated     │
    Credible / Aligned?       ├─────────────────┼─────────────────┤
                         NO   │ Potentially     │ Multimodal      │
                              │ Misleading      │ Fake Content    │
                              └─────────────────┴─────────────────┘
```

1. **Likely Authentic:** Authentic image paired with factual, verifiable reporting. Both modalities show high credibility and strong cross-modal semantic alignment.
2. **Potentially Misleading:** Genuine, unmanipulated photograph used out-of-context with a fabricated/sensational claim, or severe semantic inconsistency between modalities.
3. **Potentially Manipulated:** Visual forgery detected (facial manipulation, FaceForensics++ deepfake artifacts, or GAN/diffusion frequency grid anomalies) accompanied by neutral or unmanipulated text.
4. **Multimodal Fake Content:** Manipulated visual media coupled with fabricated, sensational, or deceptive textual claims.

## 4. Branch 1: Text Branch (RoBERTa-base & Semantic Analysis)

### 4.1 Backbone Architecture & Specifications

- **Backbone Model:** `roberta-base` (Robustly Optimized BERT Pretraining Approach).
- **Source:** Hugging Face Transformers.
- **Architecture:** 12 Transformer encoder layers, 12 attention heads per layer, 768 hidden dimension ($d_{model} = 768$), feedforward intermediate dimension of 3072.
- **Vocabulary Size:** 50,265 Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE) subword tokens.
- **Sequence Length:** Truncated and padded to maximum length $L = 256$.

### 4.2 Mathematical Formulations

#### 1. Self-Attention in Transformer Layers

Given input token embeddings $X \in \mathbb{R}^{L \times d}$, each attention head computes Queries ($Q$), Keys ($K$), and Values ($V$) via learned linear projections:
$$Q = X W_Q, \quad K = X W_K, \quad V = X W_V \quad (W_Q, W_K, W_V \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times d_k})$$

The scaled dot-product attention matrix is calculated as:
$$\text{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{Q K^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right) V$$

The contextual representation for the entire sequence is extracted from the first token position (the classification token `<s>` or `[CLS]`):
$$h_{\text{CLS}} = \text{RoBERTa}(X)_{[:, 0, :]} \in \mathbb{R}^{768}$$

#### 2. Prototype-Guided Semantic Margin Algorithm

To determine textual suspicion without relying on brittle ad-hoc heuristics, the text branch uses an empirical prototype centroid framework in 768-dimensional latent semantic space:

- $p_{\text{auth}} \in \mathbb{R}^{768}$: Prototype centroid embedding for authentic, verified, institutional reporting.
- $p_{\text{fake}} \in \mathbb{R}^{768}$: Prototype centroid embedding for sensational, conspiratorial, fabricated claims.

For an incoming input text embedding $h_{\text{CLS}}$, cosine similarities to both prototype vectors are computed:
$$\cos(h_{\text{CLS}}, p_{\text{auth}}) = \frac{h_{\text{CLS}} \cdot p_{\text{auth}}}{\|h_{\text{CLS}}\|_2 \|p_{\text{auth}}\|_2}$$
$$\cos(h_{\text{CLS}}, p_{\text{fake}}) = \frac{h_{\text{CLS}} \cdot p_{\text{fake}}}{\|h_{\text{CLS}}\|_2 \|p_{\text{fake}}\|_2}$$

The semantic distance differential (margin) is formulated as:
$$\Delta_{\text{margin}} = \cos(h_{\text{CLS}}, p_{\text{auth}}) - \cos(h_{\text{CLS}}, p_{\text{fake}})$$

The raw uncalibrated fake probability is mapped through a scaled sigmoid:
$$P_{\text{raw\_fake}} = \sigma(-\kappa \cdot \Delta_{\text{margin}}) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{\kappa \cdot \Delta_{\text{margin}}}}$$
where $\kappa = 500.0$ is the steepness scaling factor that sharpens margin discrimination.

#### 3. Grounded Stylistic & Journalistic Cue Calibration

To guard against out-of-domain vocabulary drift and reinforce journalistic credibility, the model incorporates lexical cues:

- **Sensational / Conspiratorial Cues:** Keywords indicating clickbait or fabricated claims (`shocking`, `bombshell`, `secret alien`, `urgent alert`, `miracle cure`, `deep state`, `bioweapon`, `crisis actor`).
- **Journalistic / Institutional Markers:** Terminology characteristic of vetted factual reporting (`president`, `minister`, `scientists`, `reuters`, `published`, `summit`, `conference`, `spokesperson`).

The calibrated text suspicion probability $P_{\text{text\_fake}}$ is assigned via:
$$P_{\text{text\_fake}} = \begin{cases} \max(P_{\text{raw\_fake}}, 0.82) & \text{if sensational cues} \ge 1 \text{ or breaking disaster pattern} \\ \min(P_{\text{raw\_fake}}, 0.08) & \text{if journalistic markers} \ge 2 \\ \text{clip}(P_{\text{raw\_fake}}, 0.05, 0.25) & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

#### 4. Token-Level Attribution Extraction

For explainability (XAI), the model averages attention weights across all 12 heads in the final Transformer layer ($l=12$):
$$\bar{A} = \frac{1}{H} \sum_{h=1}^{H} A_{l=12}^{(h)} \in \mathbb{R}^{L \times L}$$
The attribution score for token $i$ is the attention weight directed from the `[CLS]` token (index 0) to token $i$:
$$\alpha_i = \bar{A}_{0, i}$$
Tokens with the highest $\alpha_i$ correspond to the words most influential in establishing the text's semantic classification.

### 4.3 Input & Output Specifications

- **Input:** Raw text string (news claim or headline).
- **Internal Tensors:** `input_ids` $(1, 256)$, `attention_mask` $(1, 256)$.
- **Output:**
  - `features`: Contextual feature vector $h_{\text{CLS}} \in \mathbb{R}^{768}$.
  - `token_embeddings`: All token representations $\in \mathbb{R}^{256 \times 768}$.
  - `fake_prob` / `real_prob`: Floating point scalar $\in [0, 1]$.
  - `word_attributions`: List of token strings paired with normalized attention scores.

## 5. Branch 2: Vision Branch (EfficientNet-B0 on FaceForensics++)

### 5.1 Backbone Architecture & Specifications

- **Backbone Model:** `EfficientNet-B0`.
- **Weights:** Fine-tuned on the **FaceForensics++ (FF++ c23)** benchmark (`Xicor9/efficientnet-b0-ffpp-c23`).
- **Source:** Torchvision + Hugging Face Hub snapshot.
- **Architecture:** Mobile Inverted Bottleneck Convolution (MBConv) blocks with depthwise separable convolutions and Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) optimization.
- **Input Resolution:** $3 \times 224 \times 224$ normalized RGB tensor.
- **Feature Dimension:** 1280 (backbone average pooled) $\to$ projected to 768.

### 5.2 Mathematical Formulations

#### 1. MBConv Block with Squeeze-and-Excitation

The core building block of EfficientNet-B0 is the inverted bottleneck with depthwise separable convolutions:

1. **Expansion:** $1 \times 1$ pointwise convolution expands channel dimension by factor $t=6$.
2. **Depthwise Convolution:** $k \times k$ ($3 \times 3$ or $5 \times 5$) depthwise convolution applied per channel.
3. **Squeeze-and-Excitation (Channel Attention):**
   $$s = \sigma\left(W_2 \cdot \text{SiLU}(W_1 \cdot \text{AvgPool}(X))\right)$$
   where $W_1 \in \mathbb{R}^{\frac{C}{r} \times C}$, $W_2 \in \mathbb{R}^{C \times \frac{C}{r}}$, and $s$ dynamically re-weights feature channels.
4. **Pointwise Linear Projection:** $1 \times 1$ projection back to original channel count without activation.
5. **Residual Skip Connection:** Added when input and output spatial dimensions match.

#### 2. Visual Feature Extraction & Projection

The convolutional stem and MBConv stages produce spatial feature maps:
$$F_{\text{spatial}} = \text{Backbone}_{\text{features}}(I_{\text{RGB}}) \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 1280 \times 7 \times 7}$$

Global Average Pooling (GAP) collapses spatial dimensions:
$$f_{\text{pooled}} = \frac{1}{7 \times 7} \sum_{i=1}^{7}\sum_{j=1}^{7} F_{\text{spatial}}[:, :, i, j] \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 1280}$$

The classification logits for binary manipulation are generated by:
$$z_{\text{vision}} = W_{\text{cls}} \cdot f_{\text{pooled}} + b_{\text{cls}} \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 2}$$
$$[P_{\text{real}}, P_{\text{deepfake}}] = \text{softmax}(z_{\text{vision}})$$

To allow seamless cross-modal attention with the RoBERTa 768-dim embeddings, a linear projection aligns the representation:
$$f_{\text{vision\_768}} = W_{\text{proj}} \cdot f_{\text{pooled}} + b_{\text{proj}} \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 768} \quad (W_{\text{proj}} \in \mathbb{R}^{768 \times 1280})$$

### 5.3 Preprocessing Pipeline

Input images undergo standard ImageNet/FaceForensics++ normalization:
$$I_{\text{norm}} = \frac{\frac{I}{255} - \mu}{\sigma}$$
where $\mu = [0.485, 0.456, 0.406]$ and $\sigma = [0.229, 0.224, 0.225]$.

## 6. Branch 3: Forensic Frequency-Domain Branch (2D-FFT Analysis)

### 6.1 Algorithmic Motivation (Testing Hypothesis H2)

Generative models (GANs such as ProGAN, StyleGAN1/2/3, and Diffusion models) utilize transposed convolutions, bilinear/bicubic upsamplers, or pixel shuffle layers to expand low-resolution latent vectors into full-resolution synthetic images.

These operations inherently introduce **periodic spectral artifacts** (checkerboard patterns, Nyquist lattice peaks, and abnormal high-frequency harmonics). While these artifacts are imperceptible to the human eye and easily missed by spatial CNN filters that focus on facial semantics, they appear prominently in the **frequency domain**:

- Natural photographs strictly adhere to an isotropic power-law spectral decay: $P(f) \propto \frac{1}{f^\alpha}$ where $\alpha \approx 2$.
- Synthetically generated or edited imagery shows significant upward deviations and unnatural peaks in the upper frequency spectrum.

### 6.2 Mathematical Formulations

#### 1. 2D Discrete Fourier Transform (2D-DFT)

The RGB image is converted to a grayscale luminance channel $g(x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times W}$. The 2D-DFT computes spatial frequency components:
$$F(u, v) = \sum_{x=0}^{H-1} \sum_{y=0}^{W-1} g(x, y) \cdot e^{-j 2\pi \left(\frac{ux}{H} + \frac{vy}{W}\right)}$$

The zero-frequency direct current (DC) component is shifted to the center of the spectrum using standard quadrant centering (`np.fft.fftshift`):
$$F_{\text{shifted}}(u, v) = \text{fftshift}(F(u, v))$$

The logarithmic magnitude power spectrum is calculated as:
$$S(u, v) = 20 \cdot \log_{10}(|F_{\text{shifted}}(u, v)| + \epsilon), \quad \epsilon = 10^{-8}$$

#### 2. Azimuthal (Radial) Average Integration

To condense the 2D spectrum into a rotationally invariant 1D profile, the spectrum is integrated over concentric circular rings around the center frequency $(c_x, c_y) = (\lfloor W/2 \rfloor, \lfloor H/2 \rfloor)$:
$$r(x, y) = \sqrt{(x - c_x)^2 + (y - c_y)^2}$$
$$R(k) = \frac{1}{N_k} \sum_{(x, y) \in \Omega_k} S(x, y), \quad \Omega_k = \{(x, y) \mid \lfloor r(x, y) \rfloor = k\}$$
where $N_k = |\Omega_k|$ is the number of pixels at radial frequency radius $k$.

#### 3. Resampling & Feature Normalization

The radial profile $R(k)$ of length $K = \min(c_x, c_y)$ is resampled via linear interpolation to a fixed dimension of 128 bins:
$$f_{\text{radial}} = \text{Interp}_{128}(R) \in \mathbb{R}^{128}$$
$$f_{\text{norm\_radial}} = \frac{f_{\text{radial}} - \min(f_{\text{radial}})}{\max(f_{\text{radial}}) - \min(f_{\text{radial}})} \in [0, 1]^{128}$$

#### 4. High-Frequency Anomaly Ratio & MLP Scoring

The profile is partitioned into spectral energy bands:

- **Mid-Frequency Energy Ratio:** Mean energy in the 25%–75% band:
  $$E_{\text{mid}} = \frac{1}{64} \sum_{i=32}^{95} f_{\text{norm\_radial}}[i]$$
- **High-Frequency Energy Ratio:** Mean energy in the top 25% frequency band (Nyquist margin):
  $$E_{\text{high}} = \frac{1}{32} \sum_{i=96}^{127} f_{\text{norm\_radial}}[i]$$

A specialized 2-layer Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) scores the 128-dimensional profile:
$$s_{\text{mlp}} = \sigma\left(W_2 \cdot \text{GELU}(W_1 \cdot f_{\text{norm\_radial}} + b_1) + b_2\right) \in [0, 1]$$
where $W_1 \in \mathbb{R}^{64 \times 128}$ and $W_2 \in \mathbb{R}^{1 \times 64}$.

The final synthetic anomaly score blends the parametric MLP output with direct high-frequency spectral energy:
$$S_{\text{forensic}} = \text{clip}(0.5 \cdot s_{\text{mlp}} + 0.5 \cdot E_{\text{high}}, 0.0, 1.0)$$

### 6.3 Explainability Heatmap Generation

The 2D logarithmic power spectrum $S(u, v)$ is normalized to $[0, 255]$ and mapped through the OpenCV `COLORMAP_INFERNO` lookup table:
$$\text{Heatmap}(u, v) = \text{Inferno}\left(\text{MinMaxNorm}(S(u, v))\right) \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times W \times 3}$$
This visualizes high-frequency spikes, starburst diffraction patterns, and artificial lattice peaks for the user.

## 7. Branch 4: Cross-Modal Semantic Consistency Branch

### 7.1 Algorithmic Motivation

Out-of-context image reuse ("cheap fakes") constitutes over 60% of real-world internet misinformation. In these instances, the image is physically authentic and untouched, but the accompanying claim is entirely false. Detecting this requires evaluating whether the textual claim and visual scene semantically belong together.

### 7.2 Neural Architecture & Mathematical Formulation

The Consistency Branch maps the 768-dimensional text embedding and 768-dimensional visual feature embedding into a joint 512-dimensional metric projection space:

```
    Text Features (768-d)   ──► Linear(768 ➔ 512) ──► LayerNorm ──► L2 Normalize ──► u ∈ R^512
                                                                                         ▲
                                                                                   Cosine Similarity
                                                                                         ▼
    Visual Features (768-d) ──► Linear(768 ➔ 512) ──► LayerNorm ──► L2 Normalize ──► v ∈ R^512
```

1. **Projection with Layer Normalization:**
   $$\tilde{u} = \text{LayerNorm}(W_t \cdot f_{\text{text}} + b_t), \quad W_t \in \mathbb{R}^{512 \times 768}$$
   $$\tilde{v} = \text{LayerNorm}(W_v \cdot f_{\text{vision}} + b_v), \quad W_v \in \mathbb{R}^{512 \times 768}$$
   The projection weight matrices $W_t, W_v$ are initialized using orthogonal initialization ($\text{Xavier}$ / $\text{Orthogonal}$) to preserve distance metric properties.

2. **L2 Unit Normalization:**
   $$u = \frac{\tilde{u}}{\|\tilde{u}\|_2}, \quad v = \frac{\tilde{v}}{\|\tilde{v}\|_2}$$

3. **Cosine Similarity Computation:**
   $$\cos(u, v) = u \cdot v = \sum_{j=1}^{512} u_j \cdot v_j \in [-1, 1]$$

4. **Normalized Consistency Metric:**
   The raw cosine similarity is mapped to a strictly non-negative score:
   $$C_{\text{sim}} = \text{clamp}\left(\frac{\cos(u, v) + 1.0}{2.0}, 0.0, 1.0\right) \in [0, 1]$$

A score $C_{\text{sim}} < 0.45$ indicates severe cross-modal mismatch (e.g., disaster text paired with peaceful architecture).

## 8. Multimodal Fusion Branch & Hypothesis Testing (H1)

To evaluate **Hypothesis H1** ("Cross-modal attention fusion outperforms early and late fusion on accuracy and F1-score"), the system implements all three fusion paradigms side-by-side within the same runtime environment:

### 8.1 Primary Strategy: Bidirectional Multi-Head Cross-Attention (H1)

#### 1. Cross-Attention Mechanism

Rather than simply concatenating static vectors, cross-attention enables text features to query visual features, and visual features to query text features, discovering fine-grained semantic correlations and contradictions.

Let $f_{\text{text\_proj}} = W_{\text{tp}} \cdot f_{\text{text}} \in \mathbb{R}^{1 \times 512}$ and $f_{\text{vision\_proj}} = W_{\text{vp}} \cdot f_{\text{vision}} \in \mathbb{R}^{1 \times 512}$.

1. **Text-to-Vision Cross-Attention ($T \to V$):**
   Text representations act as Queries ($Q_t$), while Visual representations provide Keys ($K_v$) and Values ($V_v$):
   $$Q_t = f_{\text{text\_proj}} W_Q^{(t)}, \quad K_v = f_{\text{vision\_proj}} W_K^{(v)}, \quad V_v = f_{\text{vision\_proj}} W_V^{(v)}$$
   $$\text{Attn}_{T \to V} = \text{MultiHead}(Q_t, K_v, V_v) \in \mathbb{R}^{1 \times 512}$$
   $$T_1 = \text{LayerNorm}(f_{\text{text\_proj}} + \text{Attn}_{T \to V})$$
   $$T_{\text{attended}} = \text{LayerNorm}(T_1 + \text{FFN}(T_1)) \in \mathbb{R}^{512}$$

2. **Vision-to-Text Cross-Attention ($V \to T$):**
   Visual representations act as Queries ($Q_v$), while Text representations provide Keys ($K_t$) and Values ($V_t$):
   $$Q_v = f_{\text{vision\_proj}} W_Q^{(v)}, \quad K_t = f_{\text{text\_proj}} W_K^{(t)}, \quad V_t = f_{\text{text\_proj}} W_V^{(t)}$$
   $$\text{Attn}_{V \to T} = \text{MultiHead}(Q_v, K_t, V_t) \in \mathbb{R}^{1 \times 512}$$
   $$V_1 = \text{LayerNorm}(f_{\text{vision\_proj}} + \text{Attn}_{V \to T})$$
   $$V_{\text{attended}} = \text{LayerNorm}(V_1 + \text{FFN}(V_1)) \in \mathbb{R}^{512}$$

Both multi-head attention mechanisms employ $h = 8$ heads with head dimension $d_k = 512 / 8 = 64$ and dropout $p = 0.1$.

#### 2. Multimodal Fusion Vector Assembly

The attended multimodal vectors are concatenated with the forensic frequency profile and the cross-modal consistency metric:
$$F_{\text{fused}} = \Big[ T_{\text{attended}} \,(512\text{-d}) \;\|\; V_{\text{attended}} \,(512\text{-d}) \;\|\; f_{\text{forensic}} \,(128\text{-d}) \;\|\; C_{\text{sim}} \,(1\text{-d}) \Big] \in \mathbb{R}^{1153}$$

#### 3. Classification Head

The 1153-dimensional fused vector is processed by a 3-layer MLP classifier with GELU activations:
$$z_1 = \text{GELU}\left(\text{LayerNorm}(W_{c1} F_{\text{fused}} + b_{c1})\right) \quad (1153 \to 256)$$
$$z_2 = \text{GELU}(W_{c2} \cdot \text{Dropout}(z_1, p=0.2) + b_{c2}) \quad (256 \to 64)$$
$$\text{logits} = W_{c3} \cdot z_2 + b_{c3} \in \mathbb{R}^4 \quad (64 \to 4)$$
$$\mathbf{P}_{\text{raw}} = \text{softmax}(\text{logits})$$

#### 4. Grounded Calibrated Evidence Formulation

To guarantee reliable alignment with PRD Section 4.2 across all edge cases (such as zero-shot out-of-context hoaxes), the system calculates grounded evidential weights across the 4 classes:

Let:

- $p_t = P_{\text{text\_fake}} \in [0, 1]$ (Text suspicion score)
- $p_v = \max(P_{\text{deepfake}}, S_{\text{forensic}}) \in [0, 1]$ (Visual manipulation evidence)
- $c = C_{\text{sim}} \in [0, 1]$ (Cross-modal consistency score)

The evidential masses for each category are defined as:

$$
\begin{aligned}
E_{\text{Authentic}} &= (1 - p_t) \cdot (1 - p_v) \cdot (0.6 + 0.4 \cdot c) \\
\text{Penalty}_{\text{mismatch}} &= \max(0.0, 0.42 - c) \cdot 1.5 \\
E_{\text{Misleading}} &= \max\left(p_t \cdot (1 - p_v), \; \text{Penalty}_{\text{mismatch}}\right) \\
E_{\text{Manipulated}} &= p_v \cdot (1 - p_t) \\
E_{\text{Multimodal\_Fake}} &= p_v \cdot p_t
\end{aligned}
$$

The final calibrated 4-way probability distribution is normalized via:
$$P_k = \frac{E_k}{\sum_{j=1}^4 E_j + 10^{-8}}, \quad k \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$$

### 8.2 Baseline 1: Early Fusion (Feature Concatenation)

- **Principle:** Direct concatenation of unaligned raw modality features before feeding into a joint classifier.
- **Input Vector:**
  $$F_{\text{early}} = \Big[ f_{\text{text}} \,(768\text{-d}) \;\|\; f_{\text{vision}} \,(768\text{-d}) \;\|\; f_{\text{forensic}} \,(128\text{-d}) \;\|\; C_{\text{sim}} \,(1\text{-d}) \Big] \in \mathbb{R}^{1665}$$
- **Architecture:**
  $$\text{Dense}(1665 \to 256) \to \text{GELU} \to \text{Dropout}(0.2) \to \text{Dense}(256 \to 64) \to \text{GELU} \to \text{Dense}(64 \to 4)$$
- **Limitation:** High dimensionality (1665) without relational inductive bias; prone to modality dominance where the larger modality suppresses subtle signals from smaller modalities.

---

### 8.3 Baseline 2: Late Fusion (Decision-Level Aggregation)

- **Principle:** Each modality branch independently outputs a confidence score; a decision rule aggregates the scalar probabilities without joint feature interaction.
- **Combination Logic:** Uses evidential reasoning over scalar probabilities $(p_t, p_v, c)$ without access to latent feature vectors or cross-modal attention maps.
- **Limitation:** Cannot detect subtle cross-modal contradictions that only emerge when textual token embeddings directly attend to visual spatial feature maps.

---

## 9. Explainable AI (XAI) Pipeline (Testing Hypothesis H3)

Hypothesis H3 requires that detection verdicts be accompanied by transparent, human-interpretable rationales. The system integrates three complementary XAI mechanisms:

```
                       Explainable AI (XAI) Triad
                     ┌───────────────────────────┐
                     │                           │
                     ▼                           ▼
        ┌─────────────────────────┐ ┌─────────────────────────┐
        │  Token-Level Attention  │ │   2D Frequency Power    │
        │       Highlights        │ │    Spectrum Heatmap     │
        │ (RoBERTa CLS Attention) │ │    (Inferno Colormap)   │
        └────────────┬────────────┘ └────────────┬────────────┘
                     │                           │
                     └─────────────┬─────────────┘
                                   │
                                   ▼
                    ┌─────────────────────────────┐
                    │  Grounded Natural Language  │
                    │      Verdict Synthesis      │
                    └─────────────────────────────┘
```

### 9.1 Token-Level Word Attributions (Text Modality)

- **Extraction:** Computed directly from the final Transformer self-attention layer of RoBERTa.
- **Rendering:** Tokens are dynamically rendered into HTML span elements with opacity proportional to attention magnitude:
  $$\alpha_{\text{norm}} = \frac{\alpha_i}{\max_j \alpha_j}, \quad \text{color} = \text{rgba}(245, 158, 11, \text{clip}(0.75 \cdot \alpha_{\text{norm}}, 0.1, 0.85))$$
- **User Benefit:** Reviewers immediately see which specific entities, sensational claims, or verbs triggered the text suspicion score.

### 9.2 2D-FFT Power Spectrum Heatmap (Visual / Forensic Modality)

- **Extraction:** Generated by mapping the shifted 2D Fourier log-magnitude spectrum through OpenCV’s Inferno colormap.
- **Interpretation:**
  - _Authentic Photos:_ Display smooth, continuous elliptical gradients radiating outward, fading monotonically at the borders.
  - _Deepfakes & GAN Images:_ Display sharp grid-like lattice stars, periodic cross spikes, or high-energy circular rings caused by deconvolutional upsampling.

### 9.3 Natural Language Rationale Synthesis

The system parses modality scores and constructs an explanation combining evidence from all branches:

1. **Text Clause:** Specifies whether the headline exhibits verified journalistic patterns or sensational/unverified claim markers, quoting the suspicion score.
2. **Visual Clause:** Identifies whether facial regions display FaceForensics++ deepfake artifacts or if frequency analysis detected GAN upsampler grid noise.
3. **Consistency Clause:** Explains whether the text and visual content align semantically or if a context mismatch suggests the image was repurposed deceptively.
